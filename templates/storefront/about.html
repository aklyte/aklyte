About AKLYTE

A digital marketplace focused on software projects, source code and practical resources for developers and learners.

Who We Are

AKLYTE is a digital software marketplace where customers can explore and purchase ready-to-use software projects. Our aim is to make project source code and useful development resources easier to access.

The marketplace brings together projects across areas such as web development, Python, Django, MERN stack, artificial intelligence and machine learning.

Our Purpose

To provide accessible software project resources that help developers and learners study implementations, understand project structure and build their technical skills.
