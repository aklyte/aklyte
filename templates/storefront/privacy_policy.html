Privacy Policy

How AKLYTE handles information when you use our marketplace.

Last updated: November 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how AKLYTE may collect, use and handle information when you visit our website, create an account, purchase a project or contact our support team.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you use the marketplace, information may include:

  • Account details such as your name, username and email address.
  • Order and purchase information.
  • Support requests and messages you send to us.
  • GitHub username and account identifier when you connect GitHub.
  • Technical information generated when you use the website, such as browser and access information.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage customer accounts.
  • Process orders and maintain purchase records.
  • Coordinate payment processing and order status updates.
  • Provide GitHub repository access for eligible purchases.
  • Respond to customer support requests.
  • Maintain and improve website security and operation.

4. Payments

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. Payment information is handled according to the relevant provider's processes and privacy policy. Do not send card details, passwords or payment credentials to AKLYTE through support messages.

5. GitHub Integration

If you connect a GitHub account, relevant account information may be used to identify the correct GitHub user and coordinate repository invitations for eligible purchases. GitHub is a separate service and its own terms and privacy policy also apply.

6. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the marketplace, process payments, provide repository delivery or respond to support requests. We do not describe this as a sale of personal information. Any actual sharing practices should be reviewed and reflected accurately in this policy.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through the marketplace. However, no website or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as needed for account operation, order records, customer support, security, legal obligations or legitimate business purposes. Actual retention periods should be defined in accordance with our operating practices and applicable law.

9. Your Choices and Requests

If you have a question or request concerning your personal information, contact us using the business email below. We may need to verify your identity before responding to certain requests.

10. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when our services or data handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: aklyte200@gmail.com
